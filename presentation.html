The Options Dilemma & The Solution

Why traditional options trading fails retail, and how AlphaCondor fixes it
❌ The Retail Options Problem
  • Massive Capital Lockup: Single Cash-Secured Puts on blue chips require $30,000–$60,000 in cash collateral per contract for small yields.
  • Uncontrolled LLM Hallucinations: Directly giving an LLM broker access leads to catastrophic mis-sizings and illegal naked trades.
  • Rapid Intraday Gamma: 0DTE options offer explosive decay but severe tail risks without automated intraday stops.
  • Tooling Fragmentation: Retail lacks multi-leg atomic routing and continuous audit logging.
✅ The AlphaCondor Solution
  • Strict AI/Risk Decoupling: Google Gemini serves as an analyst and advisor. A deterministic Python firewall validates every order.
  • Dual-Strategy Engine: Wheel strategy on blue-chips + defined-risk 0DTE Iron Condors on SPY ($500 margin).
  • 7 Deterministic Risk Gates: Strict mathematical verification of collateral, concentration limits, and covered share backing.
  • Institutional Observability: Real-time WebSocket execution, full SQLite audit journal, CLI, and Streamlit dashboard.

Dual Options Strategy Engine

Balancing institutional stability with maximum capital efficiency
🎡 Strategy 1: The Core Wheel
  • Universe: Mega-caps & indices (AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, SOFI, SPY).
  • Phase 1 (CSP): Sells OTM puts (Δ ≈ 0.25, 30–45 DTE) on fundamentally sound companies.
  • Phase 2 (Assignment): If assigned, takes 100 shares at strike, lowering cost basis by premium collected.
  • Phase 3 (Covered Call): Sells OTM calls against held shares to harvest cash flow until called away.
  • Target Yield: 20%–35% annualized options income.
🦅 Strategy 2: 0DTE Iron Condor
  • Universe: SPY (highest liquidity broad market ETF).
  • Defined-Risk Spread: Buy Put + Sell Put + Sell Call + Buy Call.
  • 10x–20x Margin Efficiency: Required margin is capped at spread width ($500/spread) vs $30,000+ for single puts.
  • Rapid Theta Capture: Exploits extreme same-day expiration decay curve.
  • Backtest Performance: Up to 21x higher 4-day P&L potential ($2,400–$7,500 vs $350) with identical $100K capital.

The 7 Deterministic Risk Gates

Zero LLM involvement in risk decisions — pure algorithmic validation
Risk Gate Target Objective Mathematical Rule
1. Confidence Floor Model Certainty Hard rejection if AI confidence score < 70%
2. Concurrency Cap Portfolio Capacity Maximum 20 simultaneous active option positions
3. Cash Collateral Cash-Secured Check Requires 100% cash backing: Strike × 100 × Qty ≤ Available Cash
4. Concentration Cap Single Ticker Limit Position collateral cannot exceed max portfolio allocation percentage
5. Share Backing Covered Call Protection Requires ≥ 100 shares owned. Naked calls are mathematically impossible
6. Phase Consistency State Machine Rule Enforces valid transitions (cannot sell call without shares; no duplicate puts)
7. Liquidity & Spread Execution Quality Rejects options with bid-ask spread > 15% or prices ≤ $0.05

Intelligent Position Management

Automated rules protecting capital throughout the option lifecycle
🎯 50% Profit Target Take

Automatically buys to close open positions once 50% of maximum potential profit is captured. Accelerates capital turnover and eliminates long-tail holding risk.

⏳ 21 DTE Gamma Roll Rule

Option gamma accelerates sharply during the final 3 weeks. Positions reaching 21 DTE are automatically rolled out or closed to prevent assignment volatility.

🛡️ 200% Premium Stop Loss

If market movements push a position into an unrealized loss exceeding 200% of received premium, the position is immediately liquidated to protect capital.

⏰ 3:45 PM EOD Forced Exit

For 0DTE Iron Condors on SPY, any remaining open spreads are closed 15 minutes before the 4:00 PM ET close to guarantee zero overnight risk or after-hours assignment.
